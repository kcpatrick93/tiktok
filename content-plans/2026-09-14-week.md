## The week's angle: your best season is starting, and you are not posting

Two things are true at once.

**One.** Your top three products earn 73% of everything you have ever made, and they
all sell to the same person: a UK parent whose child has eczema or dry skin. Tissue
Oil £1,780, Exeskin Balm £792, Tiny Humans Big Emotions £633.

**Two.** That audience's problem is seasonal, and the season starts now. Cold outdoor
air holds less moisture, central heating strips more out of the indoor air, and
flare-ups climb through autumn to a peak in January and February
([National Eczema Society](https://eczema.org/information-and-advice/triggers-for-eczema/weather-and-eczema/),
[Eczema UK](https://www.eczema.org.uk/support/cold-weather-and-eczema)).

Your own numbers already show this. March 2026 was your best month ever at £2,428
commission, on eczema content, at the tail of last winter. Radiator foil peaked on
3 January (199,593 views, 177 sales, £398 from one video).

The catch: posting has stopped. 98 videos in June, 41 in July, 5 in August. You are
heading into the one season your catalogue is built for with the account almost dark.
Restarting takes a few weeks of consistent posting before the algorithm gives reach
back, which is why this matters in September rather than December.

**This week's job: restart posting, and plant the autumn flare-up angle early.**

## Consider ordering

**1. Humidifier (small, bedroom, quiet).** The single best fit I can find for your
audience this season. Central heating drying indoor air is a named eczema trigger by
both UK charities cited above, and a humidifier is the physical answer to the exact
problem your two hero products already speak to. It bundles: "the oil for her skin, the
humidifier for her room". Sub-£30 units carry well on TikTok Shop. Check commission
rate before committing, as small electricals often sit at 5 to 10%.

**2. Silk or bamboo pillowcase, child size.** Cheap, high margin, and it belongs in the
same bedtime routine story as the balm. Low risk test, one video.

**3. Draught excluder.** Rides the same 1 October energy story as the radiator foil, and
radiator foil already returns £86 commission per video for you, the best per-video rate
in your catalogue after Tissue Oil. Same buyer, same week, second product.

I would **not** chase the wider trend lists this week. Perfume and general beauty are
running hot on TikTok Shop right now
([Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/uk/blog/tiktok-products)), but you have no audience
authority there and you would be starting from zero against established creators. Your
edge is that you are actual parents of an actual child with eczema, in autumn.
